Sources

Every dated sentence on this site is tied to a source in the three research briefs behind it. This page gathers those works, with the URLs as they stand in the briefs. It does not add new claims.

Credit, first: the Archaeological Survey of India (Director, Epigraphy: K. Munirathnam Reddy / K. Muniratnam), whose August 2026 reading of the Pune plates gives Kumturu, 669 CE, as the earliest known epigraphical mention of the town; the Guntur District pages of the Government of Andhra Pradesh; the gazetteers and Mackenzie’s Kistna Manual; South Indian Inscriptions and Epigraphia Indica; and the 2026 reports in The Hindu, The New Indian Express, and Deccan Chronicle.
