## What each page is

### Home — `index.md`

Welcome to Kumturu. The river, the span from menhirs to 15 August 1947, and the name-chain Kumturu → Gomturu → Gunturu → Guntur. One line that Andhra State (1953) is beyond this site. Link list of the other pages. About 400–700 words.

### The name — `the-name.md`

The living chain of names. Lead with **Kumturu, 669 CE**, as the earliest known epigraphical mention (ASI, August 2026). Table of facts for the Pune plates of Vishnuvardhana II. Then the Ederu / Idern plates of Amma I (**Gomturu / Gonturu**). Then Gunturu and Guntur. Traditional *gunta / gundu / kunta* and Garthapuri as beloved local meaning, not a proved root. The district page’s 1147 and 1158 pair is mentioned only as a district mention, not as verified texts.

### Timeline — `timeline.md`

One dated table from palaeolithic / megalithic through 15 August 1947. Only dated claims that sit in the three briefs. No 1857.

### Ancient — `ancient.md`

The Krishna tract; first people; Bhattiprolu and Kuberaka; Sadas, Satavahanas, Amaravati / Dhanyakataka, Chebrolu 207 CE; Ikshvakus at Vijayapuri; Pallava–Ananda–Vishnukundin; Undavalli (ASI early–mid 7th century; popular 4th–5th / Vishnukundin dating named honestly). Closes at the Eastern Chalukyas and the 669 name.

### Medieval — `medieval.md`

Velanati Chodas, Kotas, Kondapadumatis; Palnadu (katha honoured as Guntur’s own epic; Haihayas of Gurazala in the stones; the battle as sung is later literature); Kakatiyas at Chebrolu, Motupalli, Malkapuram, and Palnad; Reddis and Kondavidu; Gajapati; Krishnadevaraya **23 June 1515**; Golconda to 1687. **Ibrahim Qutb Shah** took Kondavidu in April 1579.

### To Independence — `independence.md`

Circars; French headquarters 1752–59; Company 1788; district 1859 / 1904 / 1909; municipality; Krishna anicut; missions, college, hospital; Bapatla 1913; freedom movement through Tenali, 12 August 1942; 15 August 1947. Omit 1857. One line that Andhra State 1953 is beyond this site.

## Names in copy

Telugu and English forms as the sources use them: **Kumturu, Gomturu, Gunturu, Guntur**; **Kondavidu / Kondaveedu**; **Palnadu**; **Dhanyakataka**. Older spellings (Guntoor, Kistna, Circar, Bezwada) may appear when a source of that time is being heard.

## Hard rules for the builder

- Do not be critical of the 2026 ASI Kumturu plates. Kumturu (669 CE) is the earliest known epigraphical mention of the town name.
- Do not reopen Sewell’s doubt about Gonturu. Follow ASI and the district: Ederu / Idern = the later name Gomturu / Gonturu.
- Do not highlight a 673 vs 669 king-list tension. If handbook dates appear at all, one quiet clause only.
- Palnadu: no invented dialogue. Honour the katha; keep stones and later literature apart.
- Krishnadevaraya at Kondavidu: **23 June 1515**, not 1516.
- Kondavidu 1579: **Ibrahim Qutb Shah**, not Sultan Quli.
- Do not invent kings, dates, or temple founders. If a story is tradition, the copy already says so. Keep it that way.
- No post-1947 state politics. Andhra State 1953 is one line, then stop.
